READING
1. The speed gate is the only load-bearing gate (-0.0103 Q when forced open),
   and it is load-bearing by staying shut -- it protects the tracker from its own
   motion prior.
2. The ambiguity gate and the filter lifecycle are provably inert: byte-identical
   masks, not merely equal metrics. Once the speed gate is closed the AND-cascade
   short-circuits everything behind it.
3. Conditional post-processing is marginally harmful (+0.0008 Q when removed).
4. Every variant sits at NRE 0.035-0.038 against a baseline of 0.055, so the
   missed-detection gain lives in what all variants SHARE: the V3 chassis. Its one
   substantive difference from the baseline is MEM_WINDOW=300 sliding-window
   pruning of non_cond_frame_outputs (plus CPU offload); dam4sam_tracker.py has no
   such pruning -- verified in source.
5. Settled by the last two runs: ko_motion (no Kalman filter instantiated at all)
   is byte-identical to the full model on 375/375, and ko_chassis (memory bound
   only) is byte-identical to ko_postproc on 375/375, exactly as predicted before
   it was run. The memory window carries the whole gain; the motion module never
   executed. Root cause: a predict()/gate deadlock -- see docs/ABLATION.md.
6. ko_speed (-0.0103) is worse than ko_blend (-0.0058) despite removing fewer
   components: ko_blend's motion weight is 0.2 vs 0.5. Aggressive motion on
   ambiguous frames hurts more than mild motion everywhere.
